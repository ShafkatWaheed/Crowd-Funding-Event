Verdict: 80% Cohesive, 3 Seams Remain

The 4-group status system, filter declutter, brand token centralization, and expandable funding card transformed the app from "colorful chaos" to "intentional design system." Three seams remain: the funding results emerald break, the teal/blue duality, and the lone teal stat chip in pledges. Each is a 1-file fix.
